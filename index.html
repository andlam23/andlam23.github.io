Stock Image Fight

Stock Image Fight is a YouTube channel of short animated fights between stock photo characters: the angry guy, the fisherman, the scientist and many more, each with an escalating set of over-the-top abilities.

Watch the fights at youtube.com/@StockImageFight.

The channel's own upload tool, Stock Image Fight Uploader, uploads, titles and schedules the channel's videos on YouTube. It posts only to the Stock Image Fight channel.
